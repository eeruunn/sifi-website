PROGRAM DETAILS

Course Description

A BA in Visual Effects (VFX) and Animation with a specialization in 3D Animation is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of animation principles, digital modeling, and visual effects techniques. This program prepares students for careers in the animation industry, where they can create dynamic and engaging visuals for film, television, gaming, advertising, and other media platforms.

The program is designed to train aspiring digital artists, animators, and VFX professionals in the art and technology of visual storytelling. It combines creative development with industry-relevant technical skills, empowering students to become experts in 3D animation, visual effects, compositing, and motion graphics.

What You'll Learn

The BA VFX & 3D Animation (Honors) program has been designed to:

  • Provide comprehensive training in visual effects, 3D animation, motion graphics, and digital compositing.
  • Equip students with industry-standard software skills and practical production techniques.
  • Nurture artistic creativity, design thinking, and storytelling abilities alongside technical expertise.
  • Prepare graduates for careers in film, television, advertising, gaming, virtual reality (VR), augmented reality (AR), and new media platforms.
  • Bridge the gap between academic learning and the professional demands of the global creative industry.

Career Avenues

Film, Television & OTT

  • VFX Artist: Creating digital effects, simulations, and set extensions.
  • Compositor: Integrating CGI, live-action, and green-screen shots.
  • 3D Generalist: Handling modelling, texturing, lighting, and animation.
  • Motion Graphics Designer: Designing broadcast packages and title sequences.
  • Pre-visualization Artist: Creating animatics and Privies for directors.

Gaming & Interactive Media

  • 3D Animator: Animating characters and environments for games.
  • Environment Artist: Designing realistic or stylized 3D landscapes.
  • Character Modeller / Rigger: Creating models and skeletal systems.
  • Technical Artist: Bridging design and programming in game development.
  • VR/AR Designer: Developing immersive virtual/augmented content.

Advertising & Commercial

  • CGI Artist: Product visualizations and special effects for commercials.
  • Motion Capture Specialist: Capturing real performances for digital characters.
  • Visual Storyboard Artist: Creating visual blueprints for campaigns.
  • Digital Compositor: Merging CGI with live-action in ad campaigns.

Design & Creative Industries

  • Concept/Storyboard Artist: Designing the visual look of characters & props.
  • Texture Artist: Developing detailed surface maps for 3D models.
  • Lighting & Rendering Artist: Look development and photorealistic renders.
  • Matte Painter: Creating digital environments/backgrounds for films.
