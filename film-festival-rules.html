1

Eligibility

Open to everyone, regardless of profession, age, educational background or filmmaking experience. Professional filmmakers, students, first-time filmmakers, amateurs and members of the general public are all welcome.

Films may be submitted by:

  • Individuals
  • Groups
  • Institutions
  • Authorised production teams

The person submitting the film must have the legal authority to submit the work.

2

Production date

Only films produced or completed on or after 1 January 2025 are eligible.

3

Categories & duration

Category Duration
Short Fiction Film Below 30 minutes — any genre, style or approach
Documentary Film Below 60 minutes — people, places, ideas, events, experiences or realities
Scripted Fiction Reel Maximum 1 minute 30 seconds — created specifically for the reel format
Music Video Any language or style

Films may be made in any language. Films in languages other than English must have English subtitles.

4

Festival sections

Competition — Selected films compete for the festival's principal awards, evaluated by the festival jury.

Non-Competition — Selected films may screen as part of the official programme but do not compete for the principal awards.

The Selection Committee determines which section each selected film is placed in.

5

Submission deadline

12 September 2026. The festival may extend the deadline if necessary; any extension will be announced through official channels.

6

Entry fees

Category Entry fee
Short Fiction Film ₹1,000
Documentary Film ₹500
Scripted Fiction Reel ₹500
Music Video ₹500
All entry fees are non-refundable — regardless of selection, withdrawal or disqualification. Payment does not guarantee selection. Each film requires a separate submission and entry fee.
7

Originality & copyright

Submitted films must be original works, or the filmmaker must have obtained the necessary rights and permissions for all third-party material — music, footage, photographs, performances, archival material and more.

The festival is not responsible for copyright or other legal disputes relating to submitted films. Films found to contain plagiarism or unauthorised copyrighted material may be disqualified.

8

Selection

All submissions undergo an eligibility and technical review. The Selection Committee may consider artistic quality, originality, direction, screenwriting, cinematography, editing, sound and music, performance, visual language, creativity and overall cinematic impact.

Submission does not guarantee selection. The festival reserves the right to select films according to its artistic and curatorial vision.

9

Jury & awards

Films selected for the Competition Section are evaluated by a festival jury. The festival may also present Special Jury Awards, Jury Mentions or other recognitions at its discretion. Only Competition Section films are eligible for the principal awards.

The decision of the Jury is final and binding.
10

Screening & promotional rights

By submitting a film, the filmmaker permits the festival to:

  • Screen the selected film as part of the official festival programme.
  • Use the film's title, synopsis, poster, stills, trailer and limited excerpts for festival publicity.
  • Publish filmmaker and cast/crew information for festival promotion.

This does not transfer ownership or copyright of the film to the festival — the filmmaker retains all rights.

11

Materials required from selected films

If selected, filmmakers may be asked to provide: a high-quality screening copy, film poster, film stills, director's photograph, director's biography, film synopsis, cast & crew details, and a subtitle file where applicable.

Not providing these materials by the deadline may result in the film being withdrawn from the programme.
12

Withdrawal & disqualification

A filmmaker wishing to withdraw a film must inform the festival in writing. A film may be disqualified if:

  • False information is provided
  • It does not meet the eligibility requirements
  • It exceeds the permitted duration
  • Required subtitles are not provided
  • The filmmaker does not possess the necessary rights
  • Plagiarism or copyright infringement is discovered
  • Festival regulations are violated

Entry fees are not refunded following withdrawal or disqualification.

13

Travel & hospitality

Selected filmmakers and participants are warmly invited to attend. No TA/DA, accommodation or hospitality expenses are provided by the festival — all travel, accommodation, food and personal expenses are the participant's own responsibility.

Selection or receipt of an award does not create any entitlement to reimbursement.

14

Previous screenings & online release

There is no mandatory premiere requirement. Films previously screened at other festivals or released online may be submitted, provided they meet all other eligibility requirements.

15

Festival authority

The Festival Administration reserves the right to make decisions on eligibility, selection, programme placement, scheduling, venue, and any matters not specifically covered in these regulations. The Jury's decision on awards is final and binding. The festival may make reasonable changes to the programme, schedule or venue when necessary.

16

Acceptance of rules

Submitting a film constitutes acceptance of these Rules & Regulations. By submitting, the filmmaker confirms that they have read and accepted the regulations, hold the legal authority to submit the work, that the information provided is accurate, and that they agree to abide by all festival rules and decisions.
