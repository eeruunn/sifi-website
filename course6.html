PROGRAM DETAILS

Course Description

The BA in Film Making & Acting program at Satyajit Ray International Film Institute is designed for students passionate about the art of storytelling, performance, and cinematic production. This unique degree blends hands-on training in both film direction and acting, allowing students to explore the creative and technical sides of the film industry.

Whether you aspire to be in front of the camera or behind it, this course prepares you with the skills needed to succeed in film, television, and digital media. From script to screen, you'll learn the full filmmaking process while refining your acting craft.

What You'll Learn

This course provides a dual focus on acting techniques and film production. You'll gain practical experience in directing, screenwriting, camera operation, and editing, while also developing your on-screen presence, voice, movement, and character interpretation.

  • Master the fundamentals of acting for film and screen.
  • Learn to direct and produce short films and scenes.
  • Develop screenwriting and storytelling techniques.
  • Build a professional portfolio and on-camera showreel.

"Make impactful decisions that drive success. Lead diverse teams and enhance performance. Cultivate and implement innovative ideas. Navigate global markets and cultural dynamics."
