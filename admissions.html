ADMISSION PROCEDURE

01

APPLICATION PATHWAYS

The 50% of intake will be admitted through Allotment Process of Kannur University. The student who wishes to take admission in Management Quota has to contact the Institute office directly. The reservation policy will be followed as per the University norms.

02

DOCUMENTS NEEDED

  • SSLC Book
  • Plus Two Mark List
  • Transfer Certificate (TC)
  • Copy of Aadhaar
  • Passport Size Photo
03

FEE REGULATIONS

Detailed fee structure for tuition, hostel, production-related activities fees and caution deposit will be provided separately during admission. Each Semester Fee has to be paid before starting the class.

  • Fees shall be paid as per notified schedule.
  • Delayed payments may attract penalties.
  • Examination and special activity fees are separate where applicable.
  • Refund policies will follow institutional regulations.
04

FINANCIAL SUPPORT

Students may avail educational loans from recognized banks as per eligibility norms. The institute provides:

  • Educational Loan Guidance
  • Documentation Assistance
  • Scholarship Information
  • Financial Aid Counseling
