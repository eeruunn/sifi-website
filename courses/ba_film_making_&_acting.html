PROGRAM DETAILS

Course Description

The Bachelor of Arts in Film Making & Acting is an integrated undergraduate program that develops creative, technical, and performance-oriented skills in students. It is designed to prepare aspiring actors and filmmakers for the professional film, television, theatre, and OTT industries.

Spanning four years across eight semesters, the program provides comprehensive training in the core areas of directing, screenwriting, acting, cinematography, editing, sound design, and production. Through a blend of practical workshops, theory modules, and collaborative film projects, students learn the essentials of storytelling both in front of and behind the camera.

What You'll Learn

The program bridges the gap between theory and practice by integrating hands-on projects, workshops, and industry exposure to teach:

  • A comprehensive understanding of film production, including scriptwriting, directing, cinematography, editing, and sound.
  • Specialized training in acting techniques, voice, movement, character building, and screen performance.
  • The ability to perform multiple roles in the creative industry—as directors, actors, writers, or producers.
  • The development of artistic vision, cultural sensitivity, and critical thinking alongside professional skills.

"FROM SCRIPT TO SCREEN"

Key Highlights

  • Integrated Training: Dual focus on both acting methodology and filmmaking mechanics.
  • Project-Based Learning: Practical film and performance outputs with regular stage plays and monologues.
  • Modern Facilities: Access to studios, edit suites, sound booths, and professional filming equipment.
  • Industry Exposure: Workshops and master classes led by industry professionals and visiting artists.
  • Career Building: Final year capstone project and personal show reel development.
  • Professional Development: Real-world experience through internships, festival submissions, and showcases.
  • Cinema Studies: Exposure to global cinema with a strong foundation in Indian cinematic traditions.
  • Short Film Screenings: Regular platforms to present and critique student work.

Career Avenues

Because of the integrated nature of this course, students graduate with diverse opportunities:

Actors & Performers

Film, TV, OTT, Commercials, Theatre, Voice Acting, and Independent Theater Performers.

Filmmakers & Creators

Directors, Assistant Directors, Cinematographers, Producers, YouTubers, and Indie Filmmakers.

Writers & Editors

Screenwriters, Dialogue Writers, Script Consultants, Film/Video Editors, Post-production Assistants.

Industry & Academics

Casting Directors, Production Designers, Film Critics/Journalists, Higher Studies (MFA/MA).
