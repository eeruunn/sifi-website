PROGRAM DETAILS

Course Description

The BA Sound Recording & Sound Design program is a specialized undergraduate course designed to develop creative and technical expertise in the field of audio production, sound recording, sound design, and post-production for film and media. Sound plays a vital role in enhancing storytelling, emotion, atmosphere, and audience engagement in cinema, television, OTT platforms, gaming, and digital media.

This program provides students with comprehensive training in recording techniques, studio operations, audio editing, mixing, Foley, dubbing, live sound, and cinematic sound design using industry-standard technologies and workflows.

What You'll Learn

Through hands-on practical training, studio-based learning, and collaborative film projects, students gain real-world production experience. The program provides:

  • Theoretical Foundations: Science of acoustics, psychoacoustics, music theory, and the evolution of sound from silent films to Dolby Atmos.
  • Practical Studio Mastery: Intensive practice in sound recording studios, Foley rooms, dubbing theatres, and mixing labs.
  • Industry Software & Hardware: Hands-on training on Pro Tools, Logic Pro, and Cubase, alongside professional microphones, mixers, and audio consoles.
  • Creative Application: Developing sound design for film, television, theatre, gaming, and immersive media.

"BRING STORIES TO LIFE THROUGH SOUND"

  • Infrastructure: Industry-Oriented Curriculum with Advanced Sound Recording Studios.
  • Hands-On Practice: Practical Studio-Based Learning and Training in Audio Production.
