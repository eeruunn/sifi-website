Satyajit Ray International Film Institute, Wayanad

Inspired by the legacy of Satyajit Ray, the Satyajit Ray International Film Institute (SIFI) is a leading film institute in Kerala, offering cinema and media education in Wayanad. Our film school combines creative learning, practical filmmaking training, and industry-focused courses to prepare aspiring filmmakers and media professionals.

Rupesh Kumar

Assistant Professor | Filmmaker | Film Scholar

Rupesh Kumar is a filmmaker, film scholar, and cinema educator with over two decades of experience in film education, filmmaking, and visual storytelling. He has taught Film Studies, Cinema & Television, and Visual Communication at institutions in India and abroad, and is a frequent guest lecturer on cinema and film aesthetics. He has directed seven documentaries and two short films, with screenings and recognition at film festivals, universities, and academic forums. His industry experience includes work as an Assistant Director, Creative Director, Script Consultant, and Actor in the Malayalam film industry. A respected film critic and author of Cinemascope, he writes extensively on cinema, world cinema, film aesthetics, and visual culture.

Ranish Babu

Assistant Professor (Sound Recording and Sound Design)
