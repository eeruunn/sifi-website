Philosophy

OUR VISION

Cinema is not a window, but a mirror. At SIFI, we don't just teach the mechanics of a camera; we cultivate the eye behind the lens. Our vision is to empower the next generation of storytellers to find truth in the shadows and poetry in the silence. We fuse the heritage of classical world cinema with the tools of the future to create films that breathe.

Our Mentors

THE LEADING CAST

Kamal

Head of Directing

A true auteur who has shaped the visual language of Malayalam cinema for nearly four decades, National Award-winning filmmaker Kamal serves as our Master Mentor. With a legendary filmography spanning over 40 films—including timeless cultural milestones like Swapnakoodu, Niram, Perumazhakkalam, and Celluloid—Kamal's influence on contemporary storytelling is unmatched. As the former Chairman of the Kerala State Chalachitra Academy, his presence at SIFI offers students rare, direct access to a living master of the craft, teaching them how to balance box-office resonance with profound artistic integrity.

Dr. Rakesh Kalia

Principal

With over a quarter-century of distinguished academic leadership, Dr. Rakesh Kalia serves as the foundational anchor of SIFI. A veteran educator and former Professor at Mary Matha College, Mananthavady, he holds an MA and a PhD in Hindi. Dr. Kalia brings a profound understanding of language, literature, and institutional excellence to the institute, ensuring a structured yet deeply creative environment where future filmmakers can thrive.

Balachandran V.K

Administrative Officer

The operational engine behind SIFI, Balachandran V.K. ensures that the institute’s creative vision runs on a flawless infrastructure. Managing the complex logistics, student services, and administrative frameworks of the campus, Balachandran bridges the gap between creative execution and operational precision. His structured leadership guarantees a seamless, resource-rich environment, allowing both faculty and student filmmakers to focus entirely on mastering their craft.

Dr. Ratheesh K

Director

An intellectual powerhouse with an extraordinary, multi-disciplinary background, Dr. Ratheesh K guides the strategic and academic vision of the institute. Spanning 30 years of elite educational experience, his vast expertise cuts across Economics, Politics, Law, and Business Management—holding an unprecedented array of degrees including an MBA, LL.M, M.Phil, and PhD. Dr. Ratheesh shapes our storytellers into global thinkers equipped to handle both the art and the business of cinema.

Shiju .K

Assistant Professor (Film Direction & Script Writing)

Shiju K bridges the gap between classic mass communication frameworks and contemporary moving-image workflows. Holding a Master’s in Communication & Journalism from Kannur University, alongside a specialized PG Diploma in Television and a UGC-NET qualification, he is currently expanding the horizons of moving-image theory through his PhD research. Shiju dedicates his decade of industry and academic insight to teaching students how to direct with intent and write with visceral precision.

Jijo K Mathew

Assistant Professor (Film Making & Acting)

Cinema is defined by the human truth captured within the frame. Jijo K Mathew is a highly specialized performance mentor holding a Master of Performing Arts (MPA) in Acting from the prestigious University of Hyderabad. With 15 years of rigorous practical experience on stage and set, Jijo trains filmmakers to effectively direct actors and guides performance students to command the screen with authenticity, psychological depth, and physical precision.

Rupesh Kumar

Assistant Professor (Film Editing & DI)

Rupesh Kumar treats the edit suite as the place where a film truly comes to life. With two decades of deep industry experience, Rupesh is a master of pacing, structural rhythm, and the precise art of Digital Intermediate (DI) color grading. Armed with a Master of Communication & Journalism from Kerala University and an MA in English from Kannur University, his editing philosophy combines deep narrative psychology with high-end post-production engineering.

Ranish Babu
